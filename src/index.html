        How damage works

        Every 2 seconds the page reads Squeekchain's live status. Boss damage is the rise in measured work units this season:

        damage = sum of max(0, W(now) − W(previous))

        Both readings must fall in the same one-hour season. A drop, a gap or an unavailable status adds nothing, and an unknown status is shown as unknown, never as zero. Your points are the damage dealt after you joined, times your role and cheer boost.

        Seasons start on the hour (UTC) and run six 10-minute rounds. Boss health starts at 500 and grows to 115% of the last season's damage.
